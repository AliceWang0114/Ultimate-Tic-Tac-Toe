📋 Game Rules

1. Board Structure

The game board consists of 9 big cells arranged in a 3x3 grid. Each big cell contains 9 small cells also in a 3x3 grid. Total: 81 playable cells.

2. First Move

The first player (X or O) can play in any of the 81 cells anywhere on the board.

3. Subsequent Moves

After the first move, each player must play in the big cell that corresponds to the position of the opponent's last move within their small board.

Example: If your opponent plays in the top-right cell (position 2) of any big cell, you must play in the top-right big cell (position 2 of the 3x3 grid).

4. Freedom Rule

If the directed big cell is already won, you gain freedom to play in any unwon big cell on the board.

5. Winning a Small Board

Get 3 of your symbols in a row (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal) within a big cell to win that big cell.

6. Winning the Game

Get 3 won big cells in a row (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal) to win the entire game!
